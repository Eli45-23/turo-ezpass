# Beta Campaign Execution Plan - 21-Day Timeline

## Week 1: Research & Initial Outreach (Days 1-7)

### Day 1: Setup & Research
- [ ] Run `node prospect-finder.js all` to get all strategies
- [ ] Set up prospect-tracking.csv with initial targets
- [ ] Research NY Turo Facebook groups and join 3-5 groups
- [ ] Connect with 10 NY hosts on LinkedIn (build network first)
- [ ] Set up email templates in your email client

### Days 2-3: Prospect Identification  
**Target: Identify 50 potential prospects**

**LinkedIn (Target: 20 prospects)**
- [ ] Use LinkedIn search strategies from prospect-finder.js
- [ ] Look for hosts mentioning: tolls, EZ-Pass, bridge costs, profit margins
- [ ] Save profiles to spreadsheet with contact info
- [ ] Send connection requests with personalized messages

**Facebook Groups (Target: 15 prospects)**
- [ ] Search existing posts for toll-related complaints
- [ ] Look for hosts posting income reports or challenges
- [ ] Note active commenters who seem experienced
- [ ] Don't reach out yet - just build prospect list

**Reddit Research (Target: 15 prospects)**
- [ ] Search r/turo for toll-related posts from NY users
- [ ] Check post history to confirm they're active hosts
- [ ] Look for users complaining about toll costs or tracking
- [ ] Note usernames and recent activity level

### Days 4-5: Initial Outreach Wave 1
**Target: Contact 25 prospects**

**Email Outreach (15 emails)**
- [ ] Use "Template 1: Problem-Focused" from beta-outreach-script.md
- [ ] Personalize each email with specific details from their profile
- [ ] Send during optimal hours: 10am-2pm EST, Tue-Thu
- [ ] Track opens and responses in prospect-tracking.csv

**LinkedIn DMs (10 messages)**
- [ ] Use casual LinkedIn template from outreach script
- [ ] Only message existing connections
- [ ] Reference something specific from their profile
- [ ] Keep messages under 200 characters

### Days 6-7: Content Strategy
- [ ] Post in 2-3 Facebook groups (use group post template)
- [ ] Post on r/turo (use Reddit template)  
- [ ] Share on your personal LinkedIn about the beta
- [ ] Ask your network for referrals to NY hosts

**Week 1 Success Metrics:**
- 50 prospects identified ✓
- 25 initial contacts made ✓  
- 5+ Facebook group members gained ✓
- 3-5 email responses expected

## Week 2: Follow-up & Conversion (Days 8-14)

### Days 8-9: Follow-up Wave 1
- [ ] Follow up on Week 1 emails (use 3-day follow-up template)
- [ ] Respond to any inquiries from social media posts
- [ ] Send connection requests to new LinkedIn prospects
- [ ] Track all interactions in spreadsheet

### Days 10-11: Outreach Wave 2  
**Target: Contact 15 more prospects**

- [ ] Email 10 new prospects using different template (Solution-focused)
- [ ] DM 5 Facebook group members who commented on toll-related posts
- [ ] Engage with recent posts in target groups before messaging

### Days 12-13: Interest Conversion
**Focus on prospects who responded positively**

- [ ] Schedule demo calls with interested prospects
- [ ] Send "Address Concerns" emails to hesitant prospects  
- [ ] Provide social proof to fence-sitters
- [ ] Book 3-5 demo calls for Week 3

### Day 14: Week 2 Review
- [ ] Update prospect tracking spreadsheet
- [ ] Analyze which outreach methods got best response
- [ ] Prepare for demo calls next week
- [ ] Send final follow-ups to non-responders

**Week 2 Success Metrics:**
- 10+ positive responses ✓
- 5+ demo calls scheduled ✓
- 2-3 beta signups secured ✓

---

## Week 3: Demos & Beta Onboarding (Days 15-21)

### Days 15-17: Demo Calls
**Target: Convert 5 demos into beta users**

**Demo Structure (15 minutes each):**
- [ ] 5 min: Qualification and pain point discussion
- [ ] 8 min: Live demo with their actual toll data
- [ ] 2 min: Next steps and beta onboarding

**Demo Checklist:**
- [ ] Confirm they're active NY host with transponder
- [ ] Understand their current toll tracking process  
- [ ] Show tool with their actual EZ-Pass data if possible
- [ ] Address any technical concerns immediately
- [ ] Get commitment to beta participation

### Days 18-19: Beta Onboarding
**For confirmed beta users:**
- [ ] Send beta access credentials
- [ ] Schedule 1-on-1 setup calls
- [ ] Help them upload first CSV files
- [ ] Ensure they can generate their first test invoice
- [ ] Set expectations for feedback cadence

### Days 20-21: Final Push
**If still short of 5 beta users:**
- [ ] Leverage referrals from existing beta users
- [ ] Contact remaining high-potential prospects
- [ ] Post updates in Facebook groups about beta filling up
- [ ] Use scarcity messaging ("2 spots left") in outreach

**Week 3 Success Metrics:**
- 5 confirmed beta users ✓
- All beta users successfully onboarded ✓
- Weekly check-in calls scheduled ✓

## Contingency Plans

### If Short on Prospects (Day 7):
- [ ] Expand to NJ and CT hosts
- [ ] Use paid LinkedIn outreach tools
- [ ] Post in more niche Facebook groups
- [ ] Ask existing network for direct introductions

### If Low Response Rates (Day 10):
- [ ] A/B test different email subject lines
- [ ] Increase personalization in messages
- [ ] Focus on social media vs email outreach
- [ ] Offer stronger incentives (longer free trial)

### If Demo No-Shows (Day 15):
- [ ] Send reminder texts before calls
- [ ] Offer more flexible scheduling
- [ ] Provide pre-demo value (toll tracking tips)
- [ ] Follow up immediately with reschedule offers

### If Short of 5 Beta Users (Day 18):
- [ ] Extend beta program to 10 users
- [ ] Lower qualification requirements slightly  
- [ ] Offer referral incentives to confirmed users
- [ ] Leverage social proof from early users
