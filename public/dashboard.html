📤 Upload CSV Files

Upload your CSV files from both Turo and E-ZPass to automatically match and categorize tolls.

📋 How to Get Your CSV Files

🚗 Turo Trip CSV

  1. Go to Turo.com → Sign in
  2. Navigate to Host Dashboard
  3. Go to Trips section
  4. Click Export or Download CSV
  5. Save the file to your computer

🛣️ E-ZPass Toll CSV

  1. Go to E-ZPassNY.com → Sign in
  2. Click Transactions
  3. Select Posted Date view
  4. Click Export button
  5. Choose CSV format
  6. Click Download
